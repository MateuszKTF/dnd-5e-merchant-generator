# Item catalog — source and licence

## What the data is

`items.generated.ts` contains item names, prices and rarity tiers for four merchant
categories. It is generated by `scripts/build-item-catalog.mjs`; do not edit it by hand.

## Source

Item names and mundane prices are derived from the **System Reference Document 5.1 (SRD 5.1)**
for the fifth edition of the world's most popular roleplaying game.

The machine-readable form was taken from
[`5e-bits/5e-database`](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database) (`src/2014/en/`), which
publishes the SRD as JSON.

## Changes made to the source material

CC-BY-4.0 requires that modifications be indicated. This project changed the material as
follows, and **none of the following comes from the SRD** — all of it is this project's own
editorial work:

1. **Rarity tiers for mundane goods.** The SRD assigns no rarity to ordinary equipment.
   Each mundane item's tier (`pospolite` / `niezwykłe` / `rzadkie`) is computed from price
   terciles *within its own merchant category*, so "dear" means dear for that kind of shop.
2. **Prices for magic items.** The SRD assigns no price to magic items. Base prices are
   generated from the item's SRD rarity within bands defined by this project, deterministically
   per item id so a saved merchant never shows a different price for the same item.
3. **Rarity collapsed from five tiers to three.** SRD `Common` and `Uncommon` map to
   `pospolite`, `Rare` to `niezwykłe`, `Very Rare` to `rzadkie`.
4. **Omissions.** `Legendary` and `Artifact` items are excluded — they are plot devices, not
   merchant stock. Parent entries with rarity `Varies` (e.g. "Ammunition, +1, +2, or +3") are
   excluded in favour of their concrete variants. Items with no price and no rarity are
   excluded, since every listed item must show a price.
5. **Category assignment.** The four merchant categories are this project's grouping of SRD
   equipment categories, not an SRD structure.
6. **Pool size.** Each category is capped at 40 items, selected with a balanced spread across
   the three tiers.

## Item names are in English

SRD names are kept verbatim, untranslated, even though the interface is Polish. Two reasons:
translating ~160 item names by hand is error-prone work that the deadline does not allow, and
Polish 5e tables commonly use the English item names anyway. If this should change, translate
in a lookup layer rather than by editing the generated file, so regeneration stays safe.

## What is deliberately NOT used

Content from published rulebooks outside the SRD (Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide,
Xanathar's Guide to Everything, Tasha's Cauldron of Everything and the rest) is **not** included
and must not be added. That material is not freely licensed, and this repository is public, so
adding it would mean publicly redistributing copyrighted work. Fan compendia that mirror the
full books — 5e.tools among them — are not usable as a source here for the same reason.

This resolves PRD Open Question #2 (data source and licence).
